Moulding our nation's future through technology.

DXD builds and delivers the digital products, services, and AI capabilities that empower schools, educators, students, parents, and MOE officers — enabling MOE's digital transformation from the ground up.

Mission

What we're here to do.

We work closely with stakeholders to create better experiences for schools, educators, students, parents, and MOE officers through technology.

01

Build & deliver impactful digital solutions

We develop and maintain digital products, systems, and infrastructure that create value for MOE and schools.

Why it matters — Secure, reliable, easy-to-use services are the foundation everything else stands on.

02

Accelerate digital innovation & AI adoption

We co-create solutions with users to meet evolving needs, and drive AI adoption across MOE.

Why it matters — Partnering teams to experiment, build skills, and apply AI meaningfully to teaching, learning, and work.

03

Empower users & grow digital capabilities

We provide responsive, user-focused support to ensure smooth and effective use of technology.

Why it matters — Building digital awareness and skills so people can apply emerging technologies in their work.
