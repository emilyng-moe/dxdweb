Mission

What we're here to do.

We work closely with stakeholders to create better experiences for schools, educators, students, parents, and MOE officers through technology.

Build & deliver impactful digital solutions

We develop and maintain digital products, systems, and infrastructure that create value for MOE and schools — secure, reliable, and easy to use.

Accelerate digital innovation & AI adoption

We co-create solutions with users to meet evolving needs, and drive AI adoption across MOE — partnering teams to experiment, build skills, and apply AI meaningfully.

Empower users & grow digital capabilities

We provide responsive, user-focused support, and build digital awareness and skills so people can understand and apply emerging technologies in their work.
